## Notes (none blocks round 1)
- **Curves in a merge get flattened.** REPORT §3 says curved fills and strokes are flattened to chords
  (≤ 2 twips) when they take part in a planar split. A curve that crosses a fill comes out as many short lines,
  so bending it later bends a polyline, not a curve, and documents grow. The likely cause is that the exact
  arithmetic handles line-line intersections only. **Round 2 should intersect quadratics natively** (or re-fit
  quadratics after the split), with a test that a curve merged and then bent stays a curve.
- **My fuzz is lines only, like theirs.** Curves are where the honest fallbacks live: grazing curves, micro-cusps,
  and snap non-convergence. That path is fixture-tested, not fuzzed.
- **The feel numbers come from headless SwiftShader:** about 16.5 ms ink latency, 2.2 ms scrub frames,
  23.1 fps achieved at 24. Playback fps was measured over only 0.6 s. The real numbers are the potato's and your
  hands'.
- **No `results.jsonl`.** REPORT says it was generated by script, but the measurements behind it weren't
  delivered as data, so the browser numbers (suite 09) can't be regenerated here.
